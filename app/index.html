How every deal was checked

Each price on this page was read by our agent on Friday, Nov 27, 2026 (Black Friday) at the store itself or in a deals source that names the store — the source is printed on every card, and the full source list is on the right. Every card also carries its own check time: the first 37 deals were checked at 2:20 PM WIB, the newer deals at 5:15 PM WIB. The discount is computed from the two prices shown, so the % can never disagree with them.

What the verdict means: NEW LOW — the source called it the best price it has ever tracked. REAL DEAL — 30% or more off a real usual price. SOLID DEAL — 15–29% off, worth it if you wanted it anyway. ROUTINE SALE — under 15% off; fine, but not a reason to buy today.

Prices move all day on Black Friday. Confirm the price at checkout before you pay — a deal that sold out or moved is still listed here with the time it was checked, so you can see how fresh it is.
