How every deal was checked

Each price on this page was read by our agent on Saturday, Nov 28, 2026 (the morning after Black Friday) at the store itself or in a deals source that names the store — the source is printed on every card, and the full source list is on the right. Every card also carries its own check time. The discount is computed from the two prices shown, so the % can never disagree with them.

Price history: every card shows what the price did before today — the lowest price a tracker ever recorded, the price at an earlier sale, or an honest “no earlier price published by the source” where the tracker never published one. A deal only earns NEW LOW when the source itself called it the best price it has ever tracked.

What the verdict means: NEW LOW — the source called it the best price it has ever tracked. REAL DEAL — 30% or more off a real usual price. SOLID DEAL — 15–29% off, worth it if you wanted it anyway. ROUTINE SALE — under 15% off; fine, but not a reason to buy today.

Prices move all weekend. Confirm the price at checkout before you pay — a deal that sold out or moved is still listed here with the time it was checked, so you can see how fresh it is.
